# FlytBase BDR Hackathon Submission

The system processes raw inbound emails through an eleven-agent Gemini pipeline: parsing, company and industry research, pain-point and decision-maker analysis, MEDDPICC scoring, case-study matching, outreach creation, quality checks, and AE handoff summaries.

Frontend: React, Tailwind, Material UI, Recharts, Redux, and WebSocket updates. Backend: FastAPI, Motor/MongoDB Atlas, Gemini, and structured production logging.
